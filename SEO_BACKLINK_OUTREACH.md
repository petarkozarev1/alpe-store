## What Counts As A Good Backlink

- The page is public and indexable.
- The link points to `https://alpewear.com/shop`.
- The article/listing mentions ALPE naturally.
- The surrounding text is relevant to screens, glasses, lifestyle, studying, work, or eye comfort.
- The site is Bulgarian or has Bulgarian readers.

## What To Avoid

- Paid packages promising hundreds of backlinks.
- Foreign spam directories.
- Comment spam.
- Casino, crypto, adult, or unrelated sites.
- Exact same anchor text repeated everywhere.

## Weekly Routine

1. Contact 10 prospects.
2. Follow up with last week's prospects.
3. Add won links to the tracking table.
4. Check Search Console > Links once per week.
5. Check Search Console > Performance for queries like:
   - `alpe`
   - `alpe очила`
   - `очила за синя светлина`
   - `очила за компютър`
   - `blue light glasses`
